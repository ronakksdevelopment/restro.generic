Our Story

A kitchen built around the fire

Flame and Cloche opened with a simple idea: cook everything over open flame and let the ingredient do the talking. Every dish that leaves our hearth carries that char, smoke and warmth through to the table.

We work with regional farms and fishers, changing the menu with the seasons so what you taste is always at its best.

  • Seasonal sourcing from local farms
  • Every plate touches live fire
  • Curated wine and cocktail pairings
  • Warm, attentive hospitality

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from guests.

Do I need a reservation to dine at Flame and Cloche
Walk-ins are welcome, though we recommend a reservation for weekend evenings and parties of five or more to avoid a wait.
Can you accommodate food allergies or dietary restrictions
Yes, please note any allergies or dietary needs when booking or let your server know on arrival and our kitchen will adapt accordingly.
Is there parking available near the restaurant
Street parking is available along Hearth Lane, and valet service is offered on Friday and Saturday evenings from 6pm.
Do you offer private dining for events
We have a private room suited to celebrations and small gatherings. Contact us directly to check availability and discuss menu options.
What is the dress code
We keep things smart casual. Guests are welcome to dress comfortably while avoiding beachwear or athletic gear.
